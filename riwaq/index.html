01 — The Studio

“Riwaq” — رِواق — is the arched colonnade of classical Arab architecture: the threshold between shelter and open sky.

We are a Kuwait City design-and-engineering studio working at the intersection of architecture, interior, and structure. Every commission is private, considered, and built for the way a family actually lives.

From sculpted facades in Kifan to fluid white villas in Masayel, our work is unified by restraint: clean geometry, generous light, and materials that age with grace.
