Who we are

A discreet steward of prime property.

We are a Kuwaiti real estate company built on a family principle: acquire well, hold for the long term, and create lasting value.

Our mandate is straightforward and time-tested: leasing investment property in Kuwait's most sought-after locations, and trading land and real estate with the patience and judgment of a family office. From our headquarters in the Sharq central business district, we manage assets quietly and professionally on behalf of the enterprise we belong to.

Restraint, discretion and the long view — that is how we work.
